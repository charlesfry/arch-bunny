Rabbits thump a hind foot to warn the warren of danger.
A happy rabbit leaps and twists mid-air. It's called a binky.
Rabbit teeth never stop growing, about 3mm a week.
Rabbits eat some of their own droppings (cecotropes) to get a second pass at nutrients.
Rabbits can't vomit.
A rabbit sees nearly 360 degrees but has a blind spot right in front of its nose.
Rabbits are crepuscular: most active at dawn and dusk.
A flopped-over sleeping rabbit is called a dead bunny flop. It means they feel safe.
Rabbits purr by gently grinding their teeth.
Carrots are a treat, not a staple. Wild rabbits mostly eat grass.
Hares are born furred with open eyes. Rabbits are born bald and blind.
A rabbit's ears can rotate about 270 degrees, each one independently.
Rabbits can sprint at up to about 70 km/h.
A group of rabbits is called a fluffle.
